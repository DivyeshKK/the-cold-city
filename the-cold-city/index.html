The Record

Skills 0/5

Rules, stats & the damage formula
Base stats — basic damage 4 · basic defense 1 · HP 10 · MP 4 · move 1 · attack distance 1.

Damage formula
damage = ⌊ max(0, (basic + extra) × hits − defense) × amp × (1 + vulnerability) ⌋
Rounded down, never below 0. Defense is subtracted once per attack, not per hit — so extra hits multiply every flat bonus and cut through armor. Amp (e.g. Madness ×1.5) and vulnerability (the target's taken-damage modifier) multiply together.

Damage types — Physical: normal. Magic: ignores 50% of defense (the answer to armor). Ranged needs a long-range weapon (Bow / Staff); Melee is distance 1.

MP (the purple bar) — magic. You start with 4 and it refills at the start of every floor. Only spells spend it: Arcane Bolt costs 2, Healing costs 3, and a Magic Staff makes every spell cost 1 less. Until you draft a spell, MP simply sits full — nothing else uses it.

Skills — clear a floor, choose 1 of 3. You may carry 5; at five you must replace one. Most are passive (permanent); a few are active with limited uses per battle.

The enemies
Goon — melee. Takes the closest path to you, strikes when adjacent.
Sniper — ranged. Kites away and fires when you are outside its 5×5 watch with a clear line. Walls block it.
Mortar — artillery. Shells the tile where you last stood; its damage ignores half your defense. Step each turn to dodge it.
Warden (boss) — heavy armor (defense 3). Physical blows glance off; magic and multi-hit are the answers.

Controls — click a tile to step, click a foe to strike · WASD/Arrows step · Space end turn · R restart.
